I accidentally got hurt during Just a Pinch. Here's the challenge inscription:

Just A Pinch (15pts) (by monorail)

The flag is embedded in a hex applied to the target object, defended by two layers of protection. There is a circle of salt around the target, but it's slightly imperfect, providing suboptimal coverage. But does that matter when the other layer of defense is a welded-shut door? Break the seal through a reinforced glass window and read the flag to complete this challenge.

Diagram:

We were stumped for a long time. We figured out the door is lead-lined but not the window, but when I scryed through I couldn't see into the protection spells. So we figured it had something to do with astral projecting through the salt imperfection but none of us has projected before so we gave up and skipped it. But eventually we circled back and I had an idea.

I'm thin. The window is small enough that I could fit through if not for the (reinforced-but-not-leaded) glass. So, other than astral projection, how can you pass through a solid object?

Teleportation

The door blocks teleportation, but not the window. So teleport through the window, not through the door. We have reduced the first obstacle to a geometry problem:

My very helpful teammates held a ladder steady while I went horizontal. Getting stuck is fair game, so I prepared two misty steps so I could try to get out again.

And I teleported through. I tried to swing my legs under me to catch myself but it turns out that's really hard when you start at zero angular momentum so I hit my face on the floor. That's how I got my nosebleed.

So then I was physically in the room. The salt circle didn't matter! (To me, anyway; I'm human.) At the time I thought “so that's what the description was getting at by 'does that matter'!”. I was wrong, but I'll get to that.

The first protection was a target loophole: it was set to defend anything in the salt circle, so I spent like 20 minutes gingerly sweeping the circle over so it no longer intersected the object.

The other protection spell... did nothing. I stuck my hand straight through. This was perplexing but I ignored it.

I Identified and rescribed the hex so my teammates could decipher the flag. (I later felt foolish for this since it was fairly trivial and I should not have needed to waste the scroll but whatever.)

Then I teleported back out. I didn't have a ladder or levitation so I had to sorta plank diagonally with my forehead pressed against the window. (xenia photographed me doing this — with eyebrows furrowed and arms scrunched up to do the somatic components with just my fingers since I couldn't poke out of the window volume — but mercifully resisted the urge to post it.) I did not hurt myself going back out, despite appearing higher, because I already had my legs mostly under me. Should have done that on the way in. (But I didn't want to fizzle on a failed teleport and the ladder seemed more reliable, and I could at least show my team the hex through the window even if I got stuck....) Oh well, live and learn.
