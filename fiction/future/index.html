Your body was regenerated from scratch (or nearly) alongside modern preventive care techniques. (Preventive care is the practice of preventing disease and injury before they occur.)

You may notice missing scars. If malnutrition stunted your previous growth, you may now be taller. If an untreated hormone disorder exaggerated your previous growth, you may be a bit shorter. If your eyesight had been impaired by environmental factors, it may be better.

Damage from injuries, infections, endocrine disruption, and preventable aging should all be gone.

Ongoing care

It's important to take care of your new body. You should come in regularly for routine treatments:

  • Vaccines are injections, pills, and swabs that prevent disease. Everyone needs vaccines.
  • Hormone therapy prevents endocrine disorders. Most people need it regularly, and almost everyone needs it at some point in her life.
  • Blood tests and T scans measure your health. Ask your doctor how often you need these.

Common questions

How old am I?

Legally, your birthdate has not changed. If it's important to you to know the timeline of your body's restoration, ask your doctor.

I had long hair on my chin. Why is it gone?

You may have had an untreated hormone disorder. Check your visit summary for androgenism, a common disorder that causes hirsutism (abnormal hair growth) among other symptoms. It is now easily treatable. It can cause exaggerated height, so you may now be a bit shorter.

Where can I exercise here?

During your stay, you can hike our indoor courtyard.
