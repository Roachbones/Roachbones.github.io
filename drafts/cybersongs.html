A mysterious stranger gave away this disk at a zinefest in Wisconsin.

I brought it to a retro computer museum show, where a Wisconsin Computer Clubber named Jamie kindly greaseweazled it for me.

My friend Lynn recovered the data with me. It contains song files for MacroMind MusicWorks ver 1.1, last modified in 1988.
