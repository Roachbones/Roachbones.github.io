A mysterious stranger gave away this disk at a zinefest in Wisconsin.

I brought it to a retro computer museum show, where a Wisconsin Computer Clubber named Jamie kindly greaseweazled it for me.

My friend Lynn recovered the data with me. It contains songs for MacroMind MusicWorks, a music composition program from 1985. Converted to a modern audio format by recording a Macintosh SE emulator:

  1. Click Macintosh Garden
  2. Find MusicWorks
  3. Under MusicWorks, click the first link under “Downloads”
  4. Wait seconds for the disk to load. You should now have both and on the desktop.
  5. Open ; open
  6. File > Open
  7. Click until the CyberSongs drive is selected
